# Avishkar rules check

Checked on 20 October 2026. Compliance with one university’s annexure has **not** been verified, because the participant’s university, college, round, level, and slot or code number are not in the project files.

The slides and the poster follow the rules that are shared by the official documents below, and they leave the unknown fields blank. They do not claim that a particular annexure drawing has been copied.

## What could not be established

- The participant’s university.
- Whether this entry is a college round, a zonal round, a university final, or the state inter-university convention.
- The academic year of this entry. University of Mumbai’s site is already running the 21st Aavishkar for 2026–27. The detailed rule book retrieved in full is still the 2025–26 revised rules. A separate 2026–27 revised-rules PDF, with a new poster drawing, was not found.
- The participant’s level: UG, PG, or post-PG.
- The slot number or code number. None is invented.
- The registered project title, if the college has already filed a different title.
- The exact graphic of University of Mumbai Annexure 1 (poster) and Annexure 2 (first slide). Those pages are layout templates. Text extraction returned the headings only, not the drawn boxes.

## Documents that were read

1. University of Mumbai, Department of Students’ Development, Revised Rules and Regulations for Aavishkar, academic year 2025–26.  
   https://unimumbaidsd.com/2025-26/docs/avs_circular_6.pdf  
   Poster: 1 m × 1 m. Text legible from at least 1 m. Annexure 1 must be followed. No participant or college identity on the poster. Introduce yourself with the slot number issued on the day.  
   Podium: maximum 7 minutes, warning bell in the 6th minute, 15-second grace, then disqualification. Discussion up to 3 minutes, judges only. Annexure 2 for the first slide. No identity on the slides.  
   Poster talk: 3 minutes plus up to 3 minutes of discussion. Exceeding the poster time does not by itself disqualify.  
   Categories include Category 5, Engineering and Technology. Levels are UG, PG, and PPG. Slot number is assigned at registration on the day and must be displayed on the poster and on the first slide.

2. University of Mumbai, schedule for the 21st Aavishkar final round, academic year 2026–27, dated 9 October 2026.  
   https://unimumbaidsd.com/docs/avs_circular_7.pdf  
   This is a schedule, not a replacement template. Category 5 poster presentation is listed for Sunday 13 December 2026. Category 5 podium presentation is listed for Thursday 17 December 2026. It does not restate poster size or the first-slide drawing.

3. Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Solapur University, Aavishkar Guidelines 2025–26.  
   https://www.sus.ac.in/uploads/activities/Avishkar/Avishkar%202025%2026/PAH%20Solapur%20University%20Aavishkar%20Guidelines-2025-26%2028112025.pdf  
   Poster 1 m × 1 m. Annexure shows the words Aavishkar, the university name, Category, Code No., and Level. Identity of the participant and the college must not appear. Podium maximum 7 minutes, same warning and grace. First-slide format is Annexure 2. The extracted annexure text does not include the drawn title box in full.

4. Maharashtra state inter-university guidelines (revised with effect from 2022–23), as published by KBCNMU.  
   https://nmu.ac.in/portals/0/Avishkar/Guidelines%20for%20State-Level%20Aavishkar.pdf  
   Same poster size, anonymity, and 7-minute podium limit. A code number has four digits: category, level, then a two-digit serial. Do not reveal participant, college, or university.

5. KBCNMU poster and podium guidance file named in the brief.  
   https://nmu.ac.in/portals/0/Avishkar/Guidelines%20for%20Preparation%20of%20Poster%2C%20Model%20and%20Podium%20Presentation.pdf  
   Treated as a lead to the same state practice, not as proof of this entry’s round.

These documents agree on anonymity, a 1 m × 1 m poster, a prescribed first slide, and a 7-minute podium limit. They do not agree on the header line (which university’s name, if any, is printed as the organiser) or on whether the identifier is called a slot number or a code number.

## What the files do because of that

- No participant name, college, department, university, team name, team id, SIH logo, or GitHub or YouTube link.
- Poster page size is 100 cm × 100 cm. The exported PDF page is 100.0 cm × 100.0 cm.
- First slide fields are Title, Category, Level, and Slot / Code No.
- Category is filled as **5 — Engineering and Technology**, because that is Category 5 in every rule book above and this work is an engineering prototype. Change it if the registered category is different.
- Level is a blank line. Slot / Code No. is a blank line. Replace both in PowerPoint before the event. Do not invent the number.
- The header says “AAVISHKAR” and “Research Convention”. It does not name a university. If the applicable annexure requires an organiser line such as “University of Mumbai, Department of Students’ Development” or “PAH Solapur University”, add that line from the annexure. Do not add the participant’s own college.
- Spoken length is written for about 6.5 minutes so a 7-minute cap, if it applies, can be met. The poster is written so it can also be explained in about 3 minutes.
