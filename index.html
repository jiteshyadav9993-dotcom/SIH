SIH-26027 · CORE OBJECTIVE

AI-powered automatic block planning to maximise asset availability for train operations on Indian Railways.

The engine reads the open maintenance backlog, packs nearby jobs into one possession, and asks Google OR-Tools CP-SAT for the earliest window that keeps every train clear of the closed line. The minutes of track handed back, with the working timetable still intact, are the proof that this objective is met.

01 · FORMULA 1 · defects.py

Score every open job

Track (TMS), traction (TDMS) and signal (SMMS) defects share one backlog. Each job receives a score from 0 to 100 from safety, days overdue, an existing speed restriction, and how busy the section is. Higher scores are planned first.

02 · FORMULA 2 · defects.py

Pack them into one block

Jobs on the same running line and the same 25 kV feeder, within 3 km of each other, share one possession. Work time is the longest job plus 15 minutes to mobilise. Separate visits would have added those durations together.

03 · CP-SAT · solver.py

Fit it to the timetable

OR-Tools owns a start minute for each bundle across the 24-hour clock. The window covers the depot run, the work, the return run, and protection or earthing. The train stays off that chainage for the whole possession.

04 · ROI · metrics.py

Count the track returned

Minutes if each defect took its own block, minus minutes inside the bundle, is asset availability returned to operations. Chaos score Z is the disturbance left in the timetable. Both appear on the ribbon and in this panel.

Formula 1 · Priority urgencyPᵢ ∈ [0, 100]
$$P_i = 10\,s_i + 0.35\cdot\frac{o_i}{30}\cdot 100 + 15\,v_i + 3\,t_i$$
  • $s_i$ — safety. Severity ÷ 20, capped at 5.
  • $o_i$ — days overdue, capped at 30.
  • $v_i$ — 1 when a temporary speed restriction is already on, otherwise 0.
  • $t_i$ — traffic density of the section. This corridor uses 5 unless a section supplies its own.
A severe fracture on a busy section, already under caution, rises above a routine tamp. That order is what the planner protects first.
Formula 2 · One possession, then its lengthBUNDLE + ENVELOPE
Same line, same feeder, next defect within 3.0 km.
$$T_{bundle}=\max(T_i)+15 \qquad W=\lceil 2\,t_{transit}+T_{bundle}+B\rceil$$
$$Gain=\left(1-\frac{\sum T_{bundle}}{\sum T_{alone}}\right)\times 100$$
  • $T_{alone}$ — each defect’s own block, added up.
  • $t_{transit}$ — slowest machine, one way: tie tamper from Karjat at 35 km/h, ballast cleaner from Kalyan at 30 km/h, tower wagon from Lonavala at 40 km/h.
  • $B$ — 15 min to isolate and earth both ends on a power block, 5 min of protection on a traffic-only block.
Gain is the share of possession time handed back to trains. The envelope W is the clock time the solver must keep clear.
How CP-SAT searchesMINIMISE Z
Each bundle gets a start inside the day. The end is fixed once the envelope is known. Each train may take a delay only up to its patience.
$$\min Z=\sum_{trains} W_{type}\cdot delay+\sum_{blocks} W_{post}\cdot start$$
  • $W_{type}=300$ for premium, superfast, express and local. $2$ for freight and bankers.
  • $W_{post}$ is the “bring the block forward” weight on this screen. At 50, every later minute of the start costs 50, so a safe early gap wins.
Three policies re-solve this same model. Punctuality first keeps the passenger weight at 300. Asset first lowers it and triples $W_{post}$, so the block opens sooner. Mega block halves the passenger weight and sets the freight weight to 1, so a longer engineering window can fit. Every train stays in the safety check.
Rules the search cannot breakHARD CONSTRAINTS
1
No crash. The train is clear before the block opens, or it enters only after the block closes. The check is on the closed running line.
2
Premium and superfast stay on time. Their patience is 0 minutes, so Vande Bharat and Deccan Queen class paths keep the working timetable. Express and local may wait at most 15 minutes. Freight and bankers may wait at most 120.
3
Division machines are a pool. Two tie tampers, one ballast cleaner, two tower wagons. A machine is booked once per minute.
4
25 kV isolation is line-specific. A power block isolates that feeder’s line and earths both ends before work. The other lines keep power and stay open.
5
The possession has a fixed length. W sits inside the 24-hour clock. OPTIMAL means this Z is the best. FEASIBLE means a safe plan is in hand inside the time limit.
